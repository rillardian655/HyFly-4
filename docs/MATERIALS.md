# HyFly-4 Physically-Based Rendering (PBR) Materials

## Architectural Surfaces
- Natural European Honey-Oak: 2048x2048 procedural canvas texture with bevel grooves and bump mapping.
- White Calacatta Quartz: Subtle vein displacement and low roughness (0.28).
- Fine Plaster Walls: Neutral warm off-white with micro-roughness.
- Brushed Champagne Brass: Metalness 0.88, roughness 0.22, reflective highlights under point lights.
